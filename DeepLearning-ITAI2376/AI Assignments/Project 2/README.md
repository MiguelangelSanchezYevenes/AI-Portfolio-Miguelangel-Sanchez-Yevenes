This project introduces the concept of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and explains how Deep Learning works through simple examples and real-world applications
## Problem Statement

Many people hear terms such as AI or deep learning, but never fully understand how computers actually learn to do this. This project introduces the concept of Artificial Intelligence and explains how Deep Learning works through simple examples and real-world applications to a 11 year old kid

## Approach and methodology

The project began by defining Artificial Intelligence and explaining how computers can learn, think, and make decisions similarly to humans. One example discussed how amiibos in Smash Bros become smarter over time as they gain experience from repeated gameplay. The presentation then introduced Deep Learning as a method of machine learning that imitates the human brain using layers of neurons such as Input, Hidden and Output layers.

## Results and evaluation

The project successfully explained the relationship between Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning in a simple and understandable way. The neural network layer explanations showed how data moves through a system to produce decisions while backpropagation section shows how AI improves their accuracy.

## Your learning outcomes

I learned:

How Deep Learning imitates the structure of the human brain

How neural networks learn from data

Backpropagation in improving accuracy

How AI concepts can be explained using real-world examples
